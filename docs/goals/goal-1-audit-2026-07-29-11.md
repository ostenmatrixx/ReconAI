## Requirement gap closed locally

The PRD requires exception-queue filtering by type, status, source, amount, confidence, assignee,
age, and search. The prior implementation supported server-side status, severity, exact ID/type,
search, and stable pagination, but the remaining dimensions were absent.

The completed bounded contract is:

- source is one of `shopify`, `stripe`, `accounting_csv`, or `unclassified`; resolution expands at
  most 500 exception-held record references and joins current canonical records through the same
  explicit organization predicate under forced RLS;
- amount bounds are optional signed integer minor-unit strings, require exactly one uppercase ISO
  4217 currency, reject inverted ranges, and never compare or convert different currencies;
- confidence is deterministic review metadata derived from stored rule evidence: `fail` is high,
  `ambiguous` or `defer` is low, and absent, malformed, or other evidence is unclassified; it is not
  a probability, AI score, or measured accuracy claim;
- assignee is limited to membership-derived `me`, any assigned exception, or unassigned; no client
  membership identifier grants authority;
- age uses disjoint UTC buckets anchored to one PostgreSQL transaction timestamp; cursor v2 carries
  that instant and a SHA-256 binding over every normalized filter;
- the permissioned CSV v2 export uses identical predicates, formula protection, the existing
  10,000-row and 5 MiB limits, and a tenant audit snapshot containing the normalized filters and
  time anchor;
- low or unclassified confidence requires two independent reviewers whenever organization dual
  control is enabled.

The live PostgreSQL exercise also exposed an existing source-version evidence lookup defect in the
repository: its dynamically composed `VALUES` relation was invalid when evidence was present. The
lookup now uses a typed, bounded `jsonb_to_recordset` relation and explicit organization joins. The
same canonical record-reference projection now drives queue and detail source classification.

No migration or reconciliation rule-version change was required. No currency conversion, source
system write, provider-scope expansion, AI authority, or autonomous financial action was added.
