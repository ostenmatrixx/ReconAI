# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in ReconAI a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of
age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and
healthy community.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, respectful disagreement, constructive feedback, accepting
responsibility, focusing on community benefit, and respecting privacy and operational sensitivity.

Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or attention, trolling or insults, harassment,
publishing private information without permission, pressuring disclosure of customer/security
information, and conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers clarify and enforce acceptable behavior and may remove, edit, or reject
comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions inconsistent with this Code. Enforcement
decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and avoid exposing reporters or sensitive
information.

## Scope

This Code applies in project spaces and when representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report incidents privately to the repository owner through an existing private channel until a
dedicated conduct address is published. Reports will be reviewed promptly, fairly, and with
confidentiality appropriate to safety and legal obligations. Maintainers with a conflict must recuse
themselves.

## Guidelines

Enforcement follows the Contributor Covenant Community Impact Guidelines: correction for
inappropriate language; warning for a single incident or series of actions; temporary ban for
sustained or serious behavior; and permanent ban for demonstrated patterns or severe misconduct.
