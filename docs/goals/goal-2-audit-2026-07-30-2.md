## Focused hardening

`buildTenantObjectKey` now requires a branded organization ID and parses every segment through
bounded schemas. Cross-organization output differs, while malformed environment, organization,
opaque identifier, and traversal-like suffix inputs fail. This affects server-generated key
construction only; no stored key or database migration is rewritten.

The unused `tenantTelemetryKey` export was removed. It could produce a stable high-cardinality
tenant pseudonym even though no production call site used it. The operational alert policy now
exports its fixed forbidden-dimension vocabulary, and the tenant validator compares that vocabulary
to the manifest.
