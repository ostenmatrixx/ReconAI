shared durable-trace contracts are credible and reproducible. Goal 1 is not production-complete:
provider sandboxes, protected staging, approved retention/data disposition, representative
multi-host load and failure behavior, external monitoring/alerts, and operational restore evidence
remain open. No SLO, compliance, accuracy, savings, or production-readiness claim follows from this
audit.
